# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kissaki welcomes contributors regardless of experience, identity, disability,
background, nationality, religion, or personal characteristics. Everyone should
be able to contribute without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback, respect boundaries, and give appropriate credit.
- Keep discussions relevant, inclusive, and free of personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and obtain authorization before testing any system.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing,
spam, and publishing private information or credentials are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and public
representation of the project. Report incidents to the repository owner,
[@Zer0Dev-exe](https://github.com/Zer0Dev-exe), using a private contact method
listed on their profile. If no private method is available, request one in an
issue without identifying the affected person or sharing incident details.
Do not use public issues to publish personal evidence. Security vulnerabilities
have their own private reporting process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports impartially, protect reporter privacy as far as
practicable, and consider context and severity. They may remove content and issue
a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently ban participants.
Severe threats or privacy violations may result in immediate removal.

A participant may request reconsideration through the same reporting channel.
Maintainers involved in a report should recuse themselves where another
maintainer is available. No response-time or absolute-confidentiality guarantee
is made.
